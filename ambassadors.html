Microsoft Student Ambassadors

01 Overview

The Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA) program is a global initiative for university students to explore Microsoft technologies, build projects, and grow their communities. Ambassadors can develop practical skills using technologies such as Azure, AI, GitHub, Power Platform, and Microsoft Learn, while strengthening their leadership, mentorship, and professional skills.

The program encourages responsible AI advocacy, community impact, and digital leadership. Students can share knowledge, teach others, and support technology communities both on campus and online.

02 Key Benefits

Technical Learning

Explore Microsoft Learn, cloud technologies, AI tools, and certification opportunities. Access to specific benefits may depend on current program offerings.

Professional Growth

Build your portfolio through projects, mentorship, networking, and practical experience.

Community Engagement

Organize workshops, talks, and learning events that help fellow students discover technology.

Recognition

Showcase your contributions and achievements as you develop your technical and leadership skills.

Global Network

Connect with students and technology enthusiasts from different communities around the world.

03 Eligibility

  • Be a college or university student when applying.
  • Have a genuine interest in technology and community building.
  • Be willing to share knowledge through events, tutorials, or other activities.
  • Have or be able to create a Microsoft account.
  • Bring curiosity, enthusiasm, and a willingness to learn. You do not need to be an expert to begin exploring.

Check the official program website for current eligibility requirements and availability.

05 Activities and Engagement

  • Host technical workshops, webinars, and community events.
  • Create tutorials and share useful learning resources.
  • Take part in AI challenges, competitions, and hackathons.
  • Collaborate with other students on projects and technology initiatives.
  • Help peers discover Microsoft technologies and learning opportunities.

06 Your Impact

The MLSA program offers a way to turn your interest in technology into practical experience. By learning, building projects, and supporting your community, you can develop skills that strengthen your professional profile and prepare you for an evolving, AI-driven world.

Your journey can start with one workshop, one project, or one conversation that inspires another student to learn. Learn, lead, and innovate while connecting with a wider community of student technology enthusiasts.
